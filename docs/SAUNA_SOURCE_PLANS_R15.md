## Integration gap to decide with owner

The pinned System Cassette 01 shell is fixed at **4,572 mm outside width**, with about **4,182 mm clear**. The owner-drawn plan is about **2,000 mm outside depth**. Rotating its length onto Studio's repeated-bay axis does not solve the transverse mismatch: there would be about **2,572 mm outside-width difference**, far beyond nominal wall-thickness adjustment. The earlier generated Sauna model also varies hot-room length with size, whereas these drawings keep the hot room fixed and grow the entrance. Its optional side store is not generated by the cassette floor, exterior walls or roof. The old model view, its area/height/span screen and R14 CAD download therefore refer only to that earlier cassette study; they must not be taken as validation of the solved drawings. These are not structural construction details.

**Consultation before correcting geometry or connections:** choose whether the solved six plans should remain a 2D plan family while a narrow Cassette 01 width is researched later, or whether Studio should reserve the existing 4.572 m width and the owner wants to allocate the large residual strip in each plan. A genuinely narrower cassette and its floor/roof/bearing details belong to System, which the owner asked to pause. The building-area classification of any constructed side bay, plus actual support spacing, height, waterproofing, heater clearances and wall connections, must be rechecked once the construction geometry is chosen. No permit exemption follows from the plan study alone.
