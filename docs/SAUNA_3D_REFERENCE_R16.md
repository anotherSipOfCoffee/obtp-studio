# Owner 3D reference to System comparison · R16

The owner-provided `3d.3dm` is a **metre-unit** Rhino file with 151 objects on “Structural - Bearing” and “Interior - Furniture”. Its repeated L + side-bay shell references an approximately 6.2 × 2.0 m footprint, 3.0 m wall meshes and a 0.2 m slab envelope. It includes a transverse room partition, example door instances, separate heater/shower fixtures and sauna bench objects. It is a visual/layout reference; its meshes, wall solids and floor are not the OBTP System cassette.

The pinned System cassette controls build dimensions: 4,572 mm external transverse width, 195 mm wall depth, 220 mm structural floor joists plus 18 mm upper plywood, and 2,100 mm wall cassettes in the Studio configuration. Its optional foundation study sits below assumed grade. Studio shows structural cassette colors from System and generic gray for planning objects; generated upper and foot benches now have separate slats and supports rather than solid seat blocks, approximately 900/450 mm seat levels above the generated floor. This is a placement representation, not detailed bench construction or an approved anchorage.

The six `batches.dxf` plans have drawn outside depth about 2,000 mm and indicative ~100 mm walls. These **do not** follow the System's 4,572 mm outside width or 195 mm wall depth. Studio preserves those plans at native vector drawing scale as an owner-solved reference and labels the 4,572 mm generated model separately. No edit to the owner's room widths, doors or storage connection has been made under an invented fit. The modeled plans cannot yet be sold as six buildable cassette outputs.

A correct final generated floor plan and 3D Sauna require choosing either a narrower engineered System cassette family (including revised floor, roof and supports), or a new plan arrangement inside the 4,572 mm cassette. The 2,572 mm gross width difference is a program decision, not a drawing tolerance. Further design must resolve room widths, side storage enclosed area, door and glazing structure, sauna thermal envelope and site-specific foundation. See System `docs/cassette/FOUNDATION_AND_OPENING_STUDY.md` for foundation and aperture status.
