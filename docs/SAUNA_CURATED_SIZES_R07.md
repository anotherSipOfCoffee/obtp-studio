# Sauna R07 · curated sizes, circulation and shower strategy

Status: **research decision record, not a solved building or technical approval**. Keep Matrix as a separate geometric study/source. Preserve one System Cassette 01 structural generator for Sauna, Studio and Workshop; do not infer valid rooms from a module count.

## Finding

A raw 8–18 module slider produces many nominally legal shells but too many redundant or poorly proportioned sauna plans. Replace the primary Sauna module-count choice with a **curated whole-plan size** (S, M, L), selected after heater/bench/shower/door and occupancy fits. Keep the old count and Matrix grid for research or advanced inspection. Architecture's compact/generous selection is a catalogue of complete plans, not a continuous structural dimension slider; copy that *selection principle*, not its residential program or literal size count.

The shower is a **topology choice**, not simply a fixture visibility toggle:

| Shower strategy | Rooms / route to prove | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| Indoor dedicated | Hot room ↔ wet washing ↔ changing/rest; route and drying are checked together | First implementable baseline; existing sub-block solver expects this arrangement. |
| Exterior wall shower, no indoor wash room | Hot room, changing/rest, direct sheltered/wet transition to shower outside | Separate study branch. A real accessible shower route, frost protection, privacy, drainage, wastewater and wet/dry separation must be shown before calling this functionally valid. |
| Both | Full indoor arrangement plus optional outside rinse/cooling shower | Add after indoor baseline; the outside fixture does not replace internal washing capacity. |

An exterior shower without a dedicated enclosed room does **not** establish that its pad, screen or canopy has no effect on building area or site permissions. Check the exact built arrangement. The exterior plumbing and runoff likewise need site-specific review.

## Evidence and proportions

The [North American Sauna Society design guidelines](https://www.saunasociety.org/design-guidelines) call for convenient shower, changing and cooling/rest access and coordinated heater, door, bench and ventilation. Sauna designer Lassi A. Liikkanen's [auxiliary-space analysis](https://www.saunasociety.org/blog/2018/4/12/design-of-auxiliary-spaces-in-a-sauna) gives **washing ≈ 1.5 × hot room and dressing ≈ 2 × hot room** as advisory comparators, explicitly not legal minima. An [outdoor-shower sauna precedent](https://www.saunasociety.org/blog/2023/7/20/bringing-the-sauna-home-by-rick-watson) demonstrates feasibility, not proof that the same arrangement is functional in Lithuanian winter. A [frost-protected shower manufacturer's installation instructions](https://www.frostline.dk/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Monteringsvejledning-3080-11-2023-1.pdf) require a dry, draining well and frost-safe supply; weather suitability depends on a selected product and installation.

The existing 600 mm planning grid is a **nominal program layer** inside a wider Cassette 01 shell, not a measured finished interior. In particular its six columns cover 3,600 mm, while exterior width is about 4,596 mm. Blocks, partitions and door projections remain studies; do not size equipment from nominal rectangles alone.

These S/M/L labels indicate **complete programs**, not fixed universal capacities or an automatic 9/11/14 mapping across shower strategies. An exterior-only shell would need its own block graph and fit study. “Both” may reuse an approved indoor shell only after the exterior connection passes its own gates. None of these numbers is a permitted occupancy claim.

## Proposed product controls and validity rules

1. Sauna controls: **Size: S / M / L**, **Circulation: direct/shared / wet lobby / through (only if a two-access site brief exists)**, **Shower: indoor / outdoor wall / both**. Do not expose every Cartesian combination: define compatible complete templates and explain unavailable choices.
2. The default should be an indoor one-entry plan. One entry is also the starting assumption for a wet lobby. Keep two outside accesses as a deliberate site option; “through” means a real continuous path with two useful exterior exits, not two door icons.
3. Show the chosen layout's nominal room dimensions and generated outside-wall building area, clear shell area, calculated height, and maximum support spacing. Preserve the hard regulatory gate in the shared generator for all three program presets. An exterior canopy/screen/pad or changed roof must trigger fresh measurements and review.
4. Functional gate: required spaces exist; equipment and door candidate fit finished faces and their swings; no wet traffic forced across a dry seat; changing has believable occupancy and storage; sauna ↔ shower ↔ cooling/rest route works in all intended seasons; heater volume, bench access, moisture separation and ventilation are checked. Advisory 1.5×/2× proportions should prompt explicit assessment, not masquerade as regulations.
5. Technical gate stays false until a real wall opening system, partition assembly, shower drainage/waterproofing, electrical/heater installation, ventilation and exterior water/wastewater treatment are designed and verified. Never badge a nominal block plan as a solved building.

## Test sequence and open decisions

- Draw S, M, L indoor plans at **finished dimensions** and place actual swinging doors, bench heights, heater protection, one shower user, towels and changing seats. Compare one-entry wet lobby to the current two-entry drawing.
- Prototype outdoor-only as a *different program graph* that removes the indoor wash room and tests the exterior route. Establish year-round versus seasonal use, required privacy/shelter, water supply and wastewater concept before accepting a smaller shell.
- Prototype “both” only once the indoor plan works, showing the exterior shower as additional seasonal/cooling access. Recompute the building envelope for any built exterior additions.
- Retain a through corridor only for an explicit site route requirement; test both external doors as actual supported openings.
- Freeze the shortest **functionally passing** complete templates, then connect S/M/L selection to the same Cassette 01 generator. Re-run dimensional, sub-block, switching, DXF and rendered-plan tests. Keep Matrix outside the Sauna product catalogue.

Unresolved: intended simultaneous users; year-round exterior washing versus seasonal cooling; site access; wastewater and frost protection; finished wall thickness and actual opening structure; whether an outdoor canopy/screen/pad is built and how it is measured in the local regulatory area. The existing LT I-group dimensional envelope is a project constraint, **not a universal permit exemption**; site and land-use conditions remain separate.
