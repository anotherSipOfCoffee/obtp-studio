# Sauna layouts: precedent-led shortlist (R10, 2026-09-25)

This is a planning study, not a release of partitions, openings, equipment or wet construction. Preserve the existing Cassette 01 shell and the three-function brief: hot sauna, washing, changing/rest. The matrix remains a study.

These references establish **different spatial relationships**, not an authoritative list of three Nordic archetypes. In particular, a side corridor running the full length of this narrow OBTP shell is our own proposal and needs functional testing.

## Three OBTP layout candidates

All coordinates below are *nominal planning grid* rectangles of 600 mm, before wall finishes and door construction. The shell outside-wall area for 13 and 14 bays measured by the existing generator is approximately 35.96 and 38.72 m² respectively; none of the room-area numbers below are the regulatory building area. Candidate equipment, door swings and nominal heater range pass the present spatial solver in the stated test cases, but its status does not establish finished clearances or engineered openings.

Optional rear exit requires a second site access. | Changing 7.20, washing 5.76, hot 4.32, corridor 8.64 m², with one outside entry. | Independent room access and an optional through route; must justify the corridor as wet transition/cooling, otherwise too much area is spent on circulation. |

The existing 14-bay through-corridor trial has changing 7.20, washing 7.20, hot 4.32 and corridor 9.36 m² (nominal); its two outside entries are candidate openings only. For B, 13 bays requires relaxing the current wet-lobby minimum from 12 bays and the changing length from five to four steps. This shorter configuration passed the present door/fitting solver in an exploratory run, **but is not enabled in the product** pending full route and finished-wall review. The L changing/rest question is consequently unresolved: 8.64 m² is a reasonable *test* for B, not yet a validated replacement for the current 10.80 m².

## Filter before exposing a layout

1. The graph from every outside entry reaches changing, washing and hot room; a designated main bathing route includes washing before sauna or a deliberately specified equivalent wet transition. The optional rear door does not substitute for a usable front entry.
2. Dry clothes and seating remain away from the shower path; outside shower only is marked seasonal/technical hold until frost-safe supply and wastewater are solved.
3. Count *finished clear* dimensions for all rooms and circulation after partition and lining thickness, not just the 600 mm set-out. Place each door and its swing, benches, heater guard, shower, seat, drainage and service volume without collisions. A room called “service” needs an assigned use or removal.
4. Prefer the plan with the least dedicated circulation area **that meets the chosen access brief**; compare one-entry and two-entry briefs separately. Side corridor is a worthwhile option if independent access or a genuine through route is needed.
5. Run the generator's outside-wall area, actual height and support-spacing checks on the complete shell. Verify proposed partition penetrations and wet construction with System engineering before calling any candidate buildable.

The three choices above are a **shortlist of topologies**, not three new structural presets. Next, draw and test each at one shell size with full door and wet/dry route geometry, then permit only sizes whose completed fit passes. Keep dead-end versus through as a bounded circulation option within C rather than a fourth layout.
