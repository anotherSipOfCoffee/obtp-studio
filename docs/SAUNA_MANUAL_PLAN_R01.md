The unpartitioned clear interior estimate is 4,182 × 5,610 mm = **23.461020 m²**. The coordination rectangle inside it is six 600 mm columns × nine 600 mm rows = **3,600 × 5,400 mm**. At the rear boundary of changing/rest, each wet block has a candidate doorway edge. A front exterior entrance edge is reserved, but no opening position or width is released. No dedicated corridor is used in this layout: circulation belongs to changing/rest. An internal sauna-to-washing door is not assumed. The shared sauna/washing partition must be designed for both thermal and wet exposure before any physical partition is generated.

## Manual solving order

1. **Check the whole arrangement first.** Trace a complete route from a prospective exterior entrance through changing/rest to each wet room. Reserve wall faces for supply/exhaust, drainage and service access. Mark any edge where a cassette opening would be required. The existence of a drawn edge does not approve a cut.
2. **Solve the sauna block by hand.** Choose heater type/model and bench arrangement; draw actual heater safety envelope, bench access, ventilation paths and necessary lining thicknesses inside its allocated block. If they do not fit, resize or rearrange the *whole* plan rather than just shrinking clearances.
3. **Solve washing/bathing.** Place shower/washing fixtures, drain, waterproofed floor and transition to sauna and changing/rest. Check door operation and construction thickness at both adjoining walls. Recheck the whole route and the sauna interface after every change.
4. **Solve changing/rest and services.** Locate dry changing/seating, circulation, technical equipment and access, and an exterior entry candidate. Determine whether a separate corridor is functionally necessary in this layout; if it is, draw and validate a new whole-plan option.
5. **Reconcile physical assemblies.** Replace planning boundaries with proposed partition build-ups, actual clear dimensions and credible cassette opening interfaces. Recalculate room/door/equipment clearances, external area, generated total height and structural support spacing. Reject combinations that fail. Retain opening, heater, wet-floor and structural design holds until their details are assessed.
6. **Only then generalize controls.** Record which dimensions may change in 600 mm steps without breaking the approved spatial relationships. Test the minimum, default and maximum of each range and combinations; label any unreviewed combinations as planning studies.

## Review gates before calling this layout solved

- Measured finished room sizes and usable circulation after partitions/linings; access to each block, door swings and practical changing space.
- Selected heater installation instructions, safety distances, bench arrangement and ventilation route; no universal heater clearance is assumed.
- Wet-room waterproofing, floor fall, drain/waste connection, drying route and thermal/moisture detail at the sauna–wash partition.
- Cassette-compatible entrance and internal door construction, with structural load paths maintained; no current opening is generated.
- Regulatory dimensional calculation on the actual geometry (existing outside-wall proxy ≤50.00 m²; modelled height ≤5.00 m; support-spacing screen ≤6.00 m), plus separate legal area/classification and site review.

**Decision pending from plan review:** keep integrated circulation or develop a dedicated-corridor alternative; select electric versus wood-fired heater and the level of bathing equipment before sizing its technical zone. Those choices affect plan geometry and should be made against this full reference plan, not as isolated block defaults.
