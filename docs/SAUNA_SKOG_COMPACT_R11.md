# Sauna R11 · three compact sizes, optional rear storage, outside-only shower

Status: spatial planning candidates. This supersedes the current product choices from R09/R10; those studies remain as history. It reuses the same Cassette 01 shell and dimensional envelope gate. No residential program is introduced.

## Precedent and layout family

[Home Made Sauna's Skog](https://homemadesauna.com/products/sauna-skog) demonstrates a sauna hot room, changing area and exterior shower in a compact shell. We reuse that functional relationship, not its purchased drawings, structural system or proportions. The OBTP proposal has **one small hall at the front**, a hot room beside it, and an exterior-only shower reached from the hall side. There is no indoor shower or selectable corridor type.

The independent parameters are **size S/M/L** and **separate storage room yes/no**. When on, the shallow room is on the far side of the hot room from the entrance. It has its own rear outside door: retrieving dry stored items does not require crossing a heated room. Storage is 3.6 × 1.2 m (4.32 m²) on the nominal grid; this is a narrow full-width room, **not** a 0.4 × 0.5 m cupboard. If 4.32 m² remains too large for the brief, a genuinely smaller room needs a revised layout or a partitioned use for the remaining rear band.

The hall is only 2.16, 2.88 or 3.60 m² (nominal); it allows towels, slippers and standing indoors. With storage, the extra 1.2 m of length does not enlarge the hot room or hall. There are two proposed outside access edges without storage (main entry, shower) and three with storage (plus independent rear door). The rear store is an auxiliary non-residential space, not a bedroom or changing suite.

## Checks and unresolved details

The hot-room nominal volumes at the present 2.1 m clear height are 9.072, 12.096 and 15.12 m³. Preliminary heater references, *not installed heater specifications*, are [Harvia SW80](https://www.harvia.com/en/products/HSW800400M/the-wall-sw80-80-kw-blacksteel) at 7–12 m³ for S, [SW90](https://www.harvia.com/en/products/HSW900400M/the-wall-sw90-90-kw-blacksteel) at 8–14 m³ for M, and [Club K13.5G](https://www.harvia.com/en/products/HRKG135400/club-k135g-135-kw-steel) at 11–20 m³ for L. The L reference is a larger professional floor-mounted heater, so its footprint is drawn larger. Actual electrical supply, safety distances, manufacturer instructions, finished volume, ventilation, heater guards and bench anchorage need design.

All six nominal configurations pass the existing geometric solver's room, fixture, interior door swing, outside door edges and heater-volume screens. Generated shell height is 2.576 m and conservative roof support spacing 4.572 m. Outside-wall area is below 50.00 m². These are **dimensional checks only**; site and land-use permit conditions remain separate. The 900 × 900 mm unroofed shower pad remains outside the measured outside-wall rectangle, but future built screens, platforms or canopy may affect area classification.

The shell is 4.572 m in setting-out width, with about 4.182 m clear; the 3.6 m nominal planning grid leaves a strip between hall/storage boundaries and actual cassette wall. Doors shown at exterior wall edges are uncut proposals, not continuous finished passages. The nominal 1.2 m hall will narrow after partitions and linings. The rear storage is only 1.2 m deep before finishes. Physical access, frost protection, wastewater disposal, winter-safe footing and privacy must be solved before claiming functional or technical validation. DXF blocks, 3D accessory and linked plan are geometry studies, not fabrication documents.
