# Sauna R13 · three uses on the storage side

The six-plan correction set reuses one Cassette 01 shell for S/M/L, with optional storage off/on. The sauna and little hall stay side by side. M → L increases length by one 600 mm cassette, **without widening**. The shower remains outdoors on the hall/storage (east) side of the shell.

When storage is on, the exterior side is arranged in three parts: **1.2 × 1.2 m enclosed store** at the front/right of the hall; **900 × 900 mm outdoor shower** below it in plan; **900 × 450 mm outdoor seat** beside the shower toward the outer edge. The store has its own proposed outside door on its east face. Shower and seat remain open air; the store is an *unbuilt annex*, without generated floor, walls, roof, foundation or engineered attachment. None of the door openings is built. This is a spatial correction study, not a valid storage-on structural output.

[Home Made Sauna's Värme](https://homemadesauna.com/products/sauna-varme) is the precedent for grouping washing, dry storage and outdoor sitting around one exterior patio. Its actual plan, covered void, dimensions and construction are **not** copied. Our seat is an open-air study next to the shower and does not imply a covered patio.

The conservative screening bound is `(shell outside width + 2,400 mm) × shell outside length`; it deliberately captures open side amenities as well as the store. The legal building-area measurement and possible exemptions for uncovered exterior features require a site-specific review. The generated shell is 2.576 m high with 4.572 m conservative support spacing; no annex structure is generated, so those figures do not validate its roof or support layout.

The six CAD DXFs use millimetres and editable named equipment BLOCK/INSERTs, with shell, unbuilt store, proposed doors and exterior features on separate layers. A native DWG writer was unavailable in this environment. DXFs are correctly identified and can be opened and saved as DWG with compatible CAD software.

**Correction questions:** Should the side store join the hall wall or retain the small unbuilt gap? Does its east-facing exterior door face the intended approach? Is the seat in a usable position by the shower, or should it be farther from spray? Does S have enough outdoor access around the shower and seat? Drainage, frost protection, ventilation, heater safety, door clearances and site rules remain to be designed. Site and land-use conditions must be verified separately.
